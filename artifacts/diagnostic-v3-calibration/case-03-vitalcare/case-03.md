## B. Business reality

- Professional logo and consistent branding; clear services; own mobile-ready website with location, phone and booking CTA.
- Complete Google Business Profile and local search presence; active Instagram and occasional Facebook.
- Core information mostly consistent and meaningful positive reviews, but review acquisition is not systematic.
- Leads arrive from Google, Instagram, referrals and occasional ads; rough source knowledge but imprecise attribution.
- Online booking works, but some bookings remain manual via phone or WhatsApp.
- Contacts split between booking software and WhatsApp.
- Follow-up depends heavily on staff remembering; some unbooked inquiries followed up, others not.
- Appointment reminders automated; few other automations.
- Occasional promotions and basic analytics; no disciplined measurement routine or clear valuable-channel view.
