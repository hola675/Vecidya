## B. Business reality

- Consistent brand and functional ecommerce site with usable product descriptions/photos, acceptable mobile UX and working checkout.
- Google Business Profile for physical location, active Instagram, organic traffic, basic SEO, occasional Meta/Google campaigns, Analytics and basic ecommerce conversion data.
- Reviews sales are checked but not the full acquisition funnel; attribution incomplete and product feed inconsistent.
- Email capture exists at checkout; occasional campaigns; basic abandoned-cart automation; inconsistent repeat-customer campaigns and lifecycle segmentation.
- Reviews across platforms are not centrally managed.
- Customer service via email, Instagram and WhatsApp without unified history.
- Best sellers known, but not customer lifetime value by source; tools loosely connected.
